You have built a complete Malay version of the site and correctly told Google it exists. But the index you provide lists 244 English pages and not one Malay page — and the Malay pages themselves carry English titles. Members searching in Malay are being shown the English site.

Our priority recommendations

01

Register your Malay pages with Google

The index you supply covers only the English site. An entire language version of your website is doing no work in search, and this is the single largest opportunity here.

02

Give the Malay pages Malay titles

Your Malay pages currently present themselves to search engines in English. A member searching in Malay sees English wording and may not recognise it as the right page.

03

Replace the placeholder headings

The most prominent heading on your homepage reads “Click Here”, and two further headings are empty. These are the first things Google reads on the page.

04

Identify the cooperative formally to Google

Nothing on the site declares KATMB as a financial institution in the structured form Google uses to build organisation panels in search results.

05

Say more on the homepage

Under two hundred words introduce an organisation offering financing, pawnbroking, insurance, gold and investment. Your inner pages describe these well; the front page does not.

06

Make contact immediate

No telephone number is reachable from the homepage. For a member-facing institution, that is a barrier at the moment someone needs help.

What this means

This is a well-built institutional website. Two hundred and forty-four pages covering products, governance, history, performance, membership and welfare. The bilingual setup is correctly declared, which most Malaysian organisations get wrong. The underlying work is sound.

What is being lost is at the surface. An entire Malay site is unregistered with Google, its pages introduce themselves in English, and the homepage leads with a placeholder heading. None of this reflects the quality of what sits behind it.

Recommended next step. A technical and bilingual SEO programme — registering and retitling the Malay site first, then structured identification and homepage content.
